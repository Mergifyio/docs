title: "Automatic self-hosted runner tracking"
date: 2026-07-24
tags:
  - CI Insights
description: "Self-hosted runner and queue metrics now populate automatically, with no configuration required."
---

CI Insights now detects your real self-hosted runners on its own: any runner that runs more than one job is tracked, while single-use ephemeral runners are ignored. There's no runner configuration to set up anymore — your runner and queue dashboards populate out of the box, including for setups that were never configured before.
