Three of the options deprecated in 8.5.0 are removed here. Update any repository
that still sets them before you upgrade:

- `merge` → `allow_merging_configuration_change`. Removed outright.

- `queue` → `autosquash`. Set `autosquash` in `queue_rules` instead.

- `queue_rules` → `allow_checks_interruption`. Set `allow_checks_interruption` on
  a top-level [`priority_rules`](/merge-queue/priority) entry instead.

Two more are now fully deprecated ahead of their own removal, both replaced by the
top-level [`priority_rules`](/merge-queue/priority) section: `priority_rules`
inside `queue_rules`, and `disallow_checks_interruption_from_queues`.
