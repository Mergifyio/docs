The Slack integration gains two optional environment variables,
`MERGIFYENGINE_SLACK_CLIENT_ID` and `MERGIFYENGINE_SLACK_CLIENT_SECRET`. Setting them
does nothing on this release: posting to Slack is supported from 8.6.0, which adds the
third variable the integration needs. There is nothing to configure until you reach that
version — see [Advanced Features](/enterprise/advanced-features).
